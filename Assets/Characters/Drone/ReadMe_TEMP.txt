This folder containes drone sounds and visuals.

A 'Flying' boolean has been added to the player animator.

Drone animator also has 'Flying' as well as 'Pitch' and 'Bank' which just tilt the drone based on player move to make the movement less static.



I didn't include the movement scripts.  (Could later but it will be a while because I'll be camping. Didn't want to mess up new player controls.)

Anyways, here's some ideas for what I was playing with for controls. Feel free to your own thing, if you prefer.

Jump button makes the drone go up.
Drone slowly falls back down when not holding jump.
Holding shift stops it from drifting down maybe?
Drone can by toggled on and off anywhere (except in water) with a key press. (I used X.)
Entering the water automatilly toggles of the drone. (Use player submergence.)
High speed but low acceleration to create fun drifty feel plays nice.
